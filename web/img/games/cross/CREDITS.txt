몬스터 등굣길 (cross) art credits
=================================

cross-props.png is a small packed atlas (cropped with Python Pillow by .work/cross/build_props.py; soft
anti-alias pixels removed, nothing else changed). All source art is CC0 1.0 (public domain); no attribution
is required, but we credit the authors with thanks. The atlas rectangles are listed in web/js/games/cross.js
(const PROPS).

1) bucketR, bucketB (beach buckets), pileB, pileR (spade in a sand pile), pebL, pebS (pebbles), star1, star2 (starfish)
   Pack:    "Beach tileset" (beach_tiles_1.png)
   Author:  ChikenwingJJA
   Source:  https://opengameart.org/content/beach-tileset
   License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication), as stated on the page

2) aster (asteroid), rockS, rockT (small rocks), spark, fire1, fire2, fire3 (meteor flames)
   Pack:    "i are spaceship (16x16 space sprites)" (i_are_spaceship.png)
   Author:  bart

   License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication), as stated on the page

Downloaded 2026-11-02 from opengameart.org.

Everything else in the game is original code-drawn pixel art in web/js/games/cross.js: all four themes'
ground, roads, water/sea/lava/void lanes, bridges, rails, trains, the meteor body, cars, boats, minecarts,
UFOs, logs, surfboards, turtle shells, basalt and asteroid rafts, stepping stones, trees, palms, sandcastles,
umbrellas, boulders, steam vents, moon rocks, dishes, antennas, stars, the HUD and the chaser monster
"지각 괴물" (an original monster made of school bells and alarm clocks). The player is their own monster from
web/sprites/atlas.png, shrunk with a per-block majority filter.
