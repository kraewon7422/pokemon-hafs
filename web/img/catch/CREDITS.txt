Catch-scene backgrounds (day.png, sunset.png, night.png) are assembled by tools/draw_catch_bg.py from public-domain (CC0) packs:
- Day sky and clouds: "Sunny Land" by Luis Zuno (ansimuz) — https://opengameart.org/content/sunny-land-2d-pixel-art-pack
- Sunset clouds and green mountains: "Background Clouds And Mountains Parallax" by FabinhoSC — https://opengameart.org/content/background-clouds-and-mountains-parallax
- Night sky, moon, mountains and pines: "Mountain at Dusk Background" by Luis Zuno (ansimuz) — https://opengameart.org/content/mountain-at-dusk-background
- Grass field: "Tiny Town" by Kenney — https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town
All CC0. Credit is not required but given with thanks. Sky gradients, sun, stars and the monster's spot are drawn in the script.
Zone backgrounds (shore.png = beach/pond, desert.png, cave.png, snow.png, lava.png, plant.png = power plant, dragon.png = dragon den) are drawn entirely by tools/draw_catch_zone_bg.py (v2, 2026-11-06): original pixel art made in code, no third-party images.
